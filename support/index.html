Support

One Take is a pose camera for people who don’t know how to pose. If something doesn’t work, start here.

Contact
Open an issue at github.com/yumizhi-opc/onetake-site/issues and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later and a rear camera. The Simulator can show the pose library and outline, but live camera and auto-capture require a physical iPhone.

Camera permission

If the camera preview is black, open iPhone Settings → One Take → Camera and allow access. One Take does not request microphone, contacts, location or tracking access.

Photos

One Take asks for add-only photo library access when saving a shot. If saving fails, check Settings → One Take → Photos → Add Photos Only.

Auto-capture

Auto-capture fires after your pose matches the reference for about 0.8 seconds. If it fires too easily or not easily enough, use the manual shutter and tell us your iPhone model so we can tune it.

Capture

The camera opens on its native 4:3 frame at the normal 1× lens. Square poses preview in 1:1. Every shot saves a native full frame and an overlay crop to the in-app Gallery and iOS Photos. Review stays on the crop; use Back to Camera or Gallery.

Overlay and Watch

Tap the guide to show four handles: rotate, move, scale and reset. Auto-capture follows the moved guide. Apple Watch works as a low-resolution remote preview and shutter while the iPhone app stays in the foreground.

Privacy

No account. No ads. No tracking. Camera frames are processed on-device with Apple Vision. Read the privacy policy.
