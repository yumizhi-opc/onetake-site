Support

Camera Ready is a pose-outline camera for iPhone, with an Apple Watch remote shutter. If something does not work, start here.

Contact

Write to: jinlei34@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 business days.

What is free, and what is not

  • Starter poses are free, and the camera works fully with them. Which poses are Starter poses may change between versions.
  • Premium poses unlock with a monthly subscription, or with Camera Ready Pro, a one-time purchase. The price is shown in the app and by Apple at purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Camera Ready and cancel or change the plan. While a subscription is active you can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. You can also do this from your account page in the App Store. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

If you reinstall the app or use a new iPhone, open the store screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. If a purchase still does not appear, write to us.

Self-timer and Apple Watch

  • Self-timer: tap the timer button on the camera screen to switch between off, 3 seconds and 10 seconds, then tap the shutter. A countdown shows and the photo is taken at the end. To stop it, tap the cancel button or the shutter again. Opening a menu or leaving the app also cancels the countdown.
  • Watch: install the Camera Ready Watch app from the Apple Watch app on your paired iPhone (it may install by itself), keep Camera Ready open on the iPhone and pick a pose. Tap the Watch icon on the camera screen (it lights up when the remote is on), then open Camera Ready on the Watch. A small preview is shown and Capture takes the photo, which is saved on the iPhone. If the Watch says to agree to the terms on your iPhone, do that on the iPhone first.

Refunds, complaints and privacy requests

  • Refunds. Apple takes the payment and decides refund requests: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. If something is wrong with the app itself, write to us as well.
  • Content or likeness complaints. If you think a pose picture affects your rights (copyright, likeness or privacy), write to jinlei34@gmail.com with the subject "Content complaint". Tell us who you are and how to reach you, which pose you mean (its name in the app, or a screenshot), which right you say is affected and why, and, if you say a picture shows you, that you are that person or act for them. We aim to confirm within 2 business days and to decide within 10. The terms of use (section 16) say the rest.
  • Privacy requests. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to jinlei34@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy". We reply within 30 days. The privacy policy (section 8) explains your choices.

Camera or photos problems

If the camera is black, open iPhone Settings, find Camera Ready and allow Camera. If photos are not saved, allow adding photos there as well.

Privacy in one paragraph

There is no account, and the app has no ads and no analytics. We never receive your photos. They are saved to your device and go elsewhere only if you share them (or if you use iCloud Photos). The camera works offline; purchases, restore and links need a connection. Purchases are handled by Apple. The app does not send us your camera view. If you turn on the Watch remote, a small preview goes from your iPhone to your paired Watch over Apple's own connection. This website is hosted by GitHub, which keeps visitors' IP addresses for security. If you write to us, we keep what you send for as long as the privacy policy says. The full text is in the privacy policy.
