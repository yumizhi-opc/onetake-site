Camera Ready user guide

1. How do I pick a pose?

Tap Poses on the camera screen, then choose a pose card. Starter poses are free. Premium poses show a lock. After you have saved your first photo, tapping a locked pose opens the purchase screen.

2. How do I take a photo?

Line your body up with the outline on the live camera view, then tap the shutter yourself. Camera Ready does not take photos automatically. The eye icon hides or shows the outline. Each shot saves the full original frame and a photo cropped to the outline.

3. How does the Apple Watch remote work?

The Watch app shows a preview of the camera and taps the shutter for you from your wrist. It needs a paired Apple Watch and the Camera Ready Watch app installed from the paired iPhone app.

  • Install the Camera Ready Watch app from the Apple Watch app on your paired iPhone (it may install by itself).
  • Keep the iPhone app open and in the foreground.
  • On iPhone, tap Poses and choose a pose.
  • Tap the Watch icon on the camera, then open Camera Ready on the Watch.
  • Line up in the Watch preview and tap Capture. The photo is saved on the iPhone.
  • If the Watch says to agree to the terms on your iPhone, do that on the iPhone first.

The Watch preview is low resolution. The outline position, size and flip sync to the Watch.

4. What is free, and what is Premium?

  • Starter poses are free (40 in this version; which poses are Starter may change between versions). The camera, outline, self-timer, grid lines and Watch remote all work without paying.
  • Premium poses unlock with a monthly subscription. It renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Camera Ready Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the Premium poses in the app.
  • Prices are shown in the app and by Apple at purchase.
  • To manage or cancel the subscription, open your Apple Account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions), or tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings while a subscription is active.

5. How do I restore purchases?

If you reinstall the app or use a new iPhone, open the store screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

6. How do I redeem a code?

If you have an offer code, open the store screen, tap Redeem code and enter the code.

7. About the pose pictures

The pose pictures are AI-generated images made for the app.

8. Privacy

  • The camera view is processed on your iPhone.
  • There is no account.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • We never receive your photos. They are saved to the Photos app and to the in-app gallery on your device, and go elsewhere only if you share them (or if you use iCloud Photos).
  • The camera works offline; purchases, restore and links need a connection. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Read the full privacy policy and the terms of use.

9. Troubleshooting

The camera is black or asks for access. Open iPhone Settings, find Camera Ready and turn Camera on.

Photos are not saved. Open Settings, Camera Ready, Photos and allow adding photos. Then take the photo again.

Prices are unavailable. Check your internet connection and tap Try Again.

A purchase did not unlock anything. Tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not work, write to us at the address below.
