One Take — User Guide

One Take is a pose camera. Pick a reference pose, align your body to the live mint outline, and shoot. No account is required. Camera frames are processed on-device with Apple Vision; there are no ads.

Part 1 — Camera & App Guide

1. Open the camera

Open One Take and allow camera access. The camera opens on the main (1×) lens.

  • Portrait and Square poses use a **3:4 portrait camera frame**.
  • Horizontal poses rotate the **live camera preview and overlay 90°** into a 2:3 portrait frame, maximising the screen.
  • The app layout and pose menu stay portrait; menu cards keep their natural orientation.
  • The full camera frame is saved for every shot.

2. Pick a pose

Tap Poses (bottom of the camera). Choose:

  • **All**, **Selfie**, **Solo**, **Couple** or **Group** in the primary rail.
  • **All**, **Portrait**, **Square** or **Horizontal** in the orientation rail.
  • 72 starter poses are free: 18 from each category, six from each orientation.
  • Menu cards show the real orientation: 3:4, 1:1 or 3:2.

3. Use the overlay

The overlay keeps its own proportions inside the camera frame.

  • Handles are always visible; there is no tap-to-show/hide.
  • **Top-left** — move up/down/left/right. The guide cannot leave the camera frame.
  • **Top-right** — proportional scale. B2 starts at **0.9×** and can enlarge up to the frame; it can also shrink to 0.5×. Scaling is anchored at the guide center. Dragging toward the center shrinks; dragging away enlarges.
  • **Bottom-left** — reset.
  • **Bottom-right** — horizontal flip (mirror around the center axis).
  • There is **no rotate** gesture.
  • Portrait and Square keep the portrait preview.
  • Horizontal rotates only the live camera preview and overlay; all controls stay in the portrait layout. The camera icon turns sideways.
  • The pose picker is always portrait.
  • The guide position is only for the current shot. Choosing a different pose resets it; the pose menu default is never changed.

4. Align and shoot

  • Move yourself until the outline fits your body.
  • On preset-backed poses (576 of 740), auto-capture fires after about 0.8 seconds of stability.
  • 164 reference-only poses use the manual shutter.
  • You can always tap the shutter manually.
  • Front camera: the preview, guide and Watch preview are mirrored so left/right movement feels natural. Saved full-frame and crop images are **not** mirrored.

5. After the shot

  • The camera stays on the captured **crop**.
  • Every shot saves two images to the app Gallery and iOS Photos:

1. Native full frame (portrait 3:4; Horizontal preview is presented 2:3)

2. Overlay crop

  • Choose **Back to Camera** or **Gallery**. There is no automatic return and no retake/delete flow.
  • Gallery shows both images; the crop is the newest item.

6. Zoom

The normal lens opens at 1×. The zoom control remains available if you want to zoom; the overlay stays in its screen position and the auto-capture target follows the transformed overlay.

7. Settings

Open the gear in the camera top bar.

  • **Language** — English, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, ไทย, Bahasa Melayu.
  • **Purchases** — current plan, Restore Purchases, Manage Subscription; purchases follow the Apple Account, no One Take account.
  • **User Guide** — this guide, available offline.
  • **Permissions** — camera/photos status and Open Settings.
  • **Apple Watch** — Watch guide and foreground note.
  • **About** — version/build, Privacy, Terms, Support.

Part 2 — Apple Watch Guide

The Watch app is a remote viewfinder and shutter, not a full camera feed.

1. Install the One Take Watch app from the paired iPhone.

2. Open One Take on iPhone and keep it in the foreground.

3. Choose a pose on iPhone.

4. Tap the Watch icon on the camera, then open One Take on Watch.

5. The Watch shows a low-resolution preview with the current guide.

6. Align in the Watch preview and tap Capture; iPhone takes the photo and saves the full frame + crop.

7. Overlay move/scale/flip and front-camera mirroring sync to Watch.

8. If the iPhone app is backgrounded or the screen locks, remote preview stops. Bring the iPhone app back to the foreground.

Poses, packs and Lifetime

  • 740 poses: Selfie 113 · Solo 185 · Couple 295 · Group 147.
  • Free starter: 72 (18 per category).
  • Four category packs: Selfie 95 · Solo 167 · Couple 277 · Group 129. Each pack includes every orientation.
  • Pro Lifetime unlocks all current and future poses and packs.
  • Pro Monthly is not offered in version 1.0.

FAQ

Why is there a black bar around the camera?

The app shows a fixed portrait 3:4 camera frame. It does not crop or stretch to fill the phone screen.

Why did the outline not capture automatically?

Auto-capture needs a clear body view and about 0.8 seconds of stability. Some poses are reference-only and always use the manual shutter.

Why do I see two photos?

Every shot saves a native full frame and a cropped composition of the guide.

Where are my photos?

In the in-app Gallery and in iOS Photos. Photo access is add-only.

Does the Watch work when iPhone is locked?

No. Keep the iPhone app in the foreground and the screen awake.

How do I restore purchases?

Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases.
