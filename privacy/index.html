Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

One Take is a camera app that helps you align with a reference pose. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

One Take does not require an account and does not upload your camera feed or photos. There are no third-party advertising SDKs, no ad tracking, and no analytics SDK. We do not sell personal data.

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive transaction status and entitlement information from StoreKit; we do not receive your full payment details.

Camera and photos

The camera feed is used only to show a live preview and to detect body pose on your device. Pose detection runs locally using Apple Vision. Photos are saved to your photo library only when you tap the shutter or when auto-capture fires; they stay on your device. We never upload your camera feed or photos.

Purchases and subscriptions

Pose packs and Pro are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment and subscription management. You can restore purchases from the paywall and manage subscriptions in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Reference images and overlays

Pose reference images and their outlines are bundled with the app or generated on-device. They are not sent to a server.

Diagnostics

We do not include an analytics or crash SDK. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymised App Store and TestFlight diagnostics under Apple’s own policies; this is not linked to your identity by One Take.

Children

One Take is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: please open an issue at github.com/yumizhi-opc/onetake-site/issues.
