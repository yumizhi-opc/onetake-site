Camera Ready: Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · effective 24 November 2026

The short version

  • The Camera Ready app has no account and does not send your photos, your camera view or any personal data to us. Your own devices and Apple's services (your Apple Watch, iCloud, the share sheet, the App Store) handle some data, as sections 2 and 3 describe.
  • The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details or your name.
  • If you write to us, we get what you write. We say below how we use it and how long we keep it.
  • Our website is hosted by GitHub. GitHub keeps visitors' IP addresses for security.

2. What the app does on your iPhone

We do not receive anything described in this section.

  • Camera. The app uses the camera to show a live view with the pose outline on top. The live view is not recorded and not sent to us. In this version the app does not analyse the camera view to find people or body poses.
  • Photos you take. When you take a photo (with the shutter button, the self-timer you started, or the Capture button on your Apple Watch), the app saves it to your photo library and to the app's own gallery on your iPhone. It saves a full frame and a second image cropped to the area inside the outline; the outline itself is not drawn on either image. The photo-library permission is "add only": the app can add photos but cannot see the other photos in your library. You can remove a photo from the app's gallery; that does not remove it from your photo library.
  • Your phone's own copies. Your iPhone settings may copy the photos, and the app's gallery, to other places, for example iCloud Photos or a device backup. That is your choice and Apple's service, not ours.
  • Apple Watch. If you turn on the Watch remote, the iPhone sends a small preview picture, about three per second, to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's own phone-to-watch connection, so you can line up your shot. The Watch sends back a "capture" command. The Watch app keeps the preview in memory only. Nothing goes to us.
  • Settings the app remembers. Your language, the self-timer setting, whether the grid is on and the last pose you chose. The number of photos you have saved, whether you have saved a first photo and which app version last showed the rating prompt: the app uses these to decide when Apple's rating prompt may appear and when tapping a locked pose opens the purchase screen. Which poses you opened by sharing the app. If you subscribe: which subscription Apple last confirmed, when its paid period ends and when the app last checked, so that Premium poses stay open for a short time while your iPhone cannot reach the App Store. The terms you accepted: their version, the date and time, the language they were shown in, the app version, and whether you agreed at a photo or at a purchase. All of this stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Apple's services. Buying, restoring, the prices shown and the rating prompt are handled by Apple's services, which connect from your iPhone to Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The rating prompt is Apple's own system dialog; we do not see your answer. The camera works without a network; buying, restoring and opening links need one. What Apple later reports to us about purchases is in section 3.
  • Sharing. If you share a photo or the app's link, you use the iPhone share sheet. The app does not see where you send it. If you share the app to open the share poses, we do not collect or receive anything about the share: not whether you shared, where, or with whom.
  • What the app does not use. The app does not ask for or use your location, microphone or contacts, and does not track you.
  • Links. Settings can open our website pages, App Store pages and iPhone Settings. Opening a web page or an App Store link sends a normal web request from your browser or the App Store app to that site (section 5 says what GitHub logs). Those pages are run by others and have their own privacy rules.

If you choose to share app analytics or crash data with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us reports without personal details. We use them only to fix and improve the app.

3. Purchases

You can buy Camera Ready Pro, a one-time purchase that does not renew, and Camera Ready Premium Monthly, a subscription that renews every month until you cancel it. Apple takes the payment under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your card or payment details. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address, and the app gives us no access to them. The app asks Apple on your iPhone which purchases and subscriptions your Apple Account has, so that it can unlock them; nothing about this goes to us. If you use an offer code, Apple's reports show us the name and type of the offer, the product, the country and the date of the sale.

If you subscribe, Apple gives us reports about subscriptions. For each event (a sign-up, a renewal, a cancellation, a refund) the report includes the dates, the subscription's name and length, the offer's name and type, the price and currency, our proceeds, whether it was refunded, your device type, your App Store country and a random Subscriber ID that is unique to you and to us. It has no name or email field. Apple deletes the ID if you cancel all your subscriptions with us and do not come back within 180 days; reports we have already downloaded keep the ID for 6 years. We cannot contact you from these reports. We use them to count sales, to check that the subscription works and to match refunds. We keep the reports for 6 years for accounts and tax.

4. If you write to us

When you email jinlei34@gmail.com, or use the "Send feedback" link in the app (it opens your own email app with a draft you can change and send), we receive your email address, your name if you give it, your message and anything you attach. The feedback link adds the app version and iOS version to the draft.

  • Why: to answer you, to fix problems, and to keep a record of a complaint or request. In the UK, Switzerland and Norway, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering and supporting users, or your request, or, for content complaints, our need to handle the claim.
  • How long: we delete a conversation 12 months after the last message, unless we need it for a legal claim or a content complaint under the terms of use, in which case we keep it until the matter is closed and for 3 years after. After we delete a conversation we keep a short record that a request was made and answered (date, type and outcome, without your address or message).
  • Who sees it: we do. Our email provider, Gmail (a Google service), stores the mail on our behalf, on its servers, which may be in other countries than yours. We do not sell it or share it for advertising. We may give it to a court or authority if the law requires.
  • Giving us this information is voluntary. If you do not write to us, we receive nothing. Without an email address we cannot answer you.
  • Please do not send photos of other people, or sensitive details, unless you need to.

We add no cookies, no analytics and no outside scripts or fonts to the pages. If we move the site or add a tool, we will change this policy first.

6. Sharing, selling and transfers

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising or tracking. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app. The app itself sends no personal data to us or to any third-party service of ours; Apple's services and your browser behave as section 2 describes. Our email provider and GitHub handle the information described above under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Children

Camera Ready is not aimed at children under 13, and Apple sets its own minimum age for an Apple Account. The app itself collects no personal data from anyone, including children. What we receive about a person is only what Apple's subscription reports contain (section 3: no name or email, a random Subscriber ID) and anything you write to us (section 4). If a child has written to us, tell us and we will delete the message.

8. Your choices and rights

In the app. You control what the app can reach. In iPhone Settings > Camera Ready you can turn Camera and Photos access on or off at any time. Without camera access, the app cannot show the live view. Deleting the app removes the app's stored settings and its gallery from the phone.

With us. Whatever country you live in, you can write to jinlei34@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you (normally only an email conversation; if you subscribed, we may also hold report rows with a random Subscriber ID, which we cannot find by your name or email: tell us the date and the Apple order details and we will try), to ask us to correct or delete it, to ask us to stop using it, or to ask for a copy. We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to prove you are the person concerned. We do not charge for this. There is no automated decision-making.

Photos. We do not hold your photos and cannot remove them. If someone photographed you with Camera Ready and shared the photo, ask that person.
